Important Instructions
Participation in Convocation
Merely providing consent through the Google Form does not confirm participation in the Convocation ceremony. Completion of the registration process and attendance in the rehearsal are mandatory for participation in person.
Graduands are advised to plan their travel accordingly to ensure timely arrival. No exceptions shall be made in case of non-compliance with the above instructions.
Graduation Requirement and No Dues
Students who have successfully completed all academic requirements, including submission of the required project reports, thesis, dissertation, etc., shall have their graduation requirements officially confirmed online by the respective Batch Faculty Advisor, DUGC/DPGC/DRC, and Department, with final approval from the concerned Head of Department (HoD).
Only students who have completed all graduation requirements, cleared all dues, paid the prescribed Convocation fee, and completed final document verification shall be eligible to be convocated and issued the degree/diploma certificate.
This notice shall not be treated as proof of award of degree or confirmation of convocation eligibility.
No Exception for Inclusion of Name After Registration Closure
After closure of Convocation registration on
05 June 2026
, the Office of Academic Affairs shall prepare the final list of graduates receiving degrees in person and share the same with the respective HoDs and Faculty Advisors.
The final list shall also be used to confirm seat allocation for each graduate in coordination with the Convocation organizing team by
12:00 PM on 05 June 2026
.
The list shall be frozen by
12:00 PM on 05 June 2026
to facilitate necessary arrangements by the Office of Academic Affairs.
This finalized list shall be used during the Convocation rehearsal by the Faculty Advisor. Since the degree distribution will be conducted simultaneously from both sides of the stage, attendance in the rehearsal is mandatory to confirm seating arrangements and understand the process of receiving the degree.
No request for inclusion of names in the physical registration list shall be entertained after
12:00 PM on 05 June 2026
.
Faculty Advisors shall take attendance of students at the specified reporting time during both the rehearsal and the Convocation ceremony. Students reporting late may have their names removed from the on-stage degree distribution list. In such cases, the degree may be collected later from the Office of Academic Affairs after completion of the Convocation ceremony.
Convocation Hall and Guest Arrangements
Convocation Day is a significant occasion for graduands and their families. However, due to the magnitude, time-sensitive nature of the ceremony, and limited seating capacity in the Convocation Hall, only graduands scheduled for the respective session shall be permitted entry into the Convocation Hall.
The final list shall also be used to confirm seat allocation for each graduate in coordination with the Convocation organizing team by 12:00 PM on 05 June 2026.
Arrangements for live streaming of the ceremony shall be made in other lecture halls within the building (except Convocation Hall) for accompanying guests, subject to limited seating availability.
Graduands are requested to plan their travel and accompanying arrangements accordingly.
Degree Dispatch for In-Absentia Graduands
The Institute shall dispatch degrees to graduates who are unable to attend the Convocation ceremony and receive their degrees in absentia through a designated postal arrangement.
Applicable postage and handling charges, if any, shall be borne by the concerned graduate.
Detailed instructions regarding reporting time, venue arrangements, registration procedure, and other ceremonial guidelines shall be updated on the 12th Convocation 2026 website. Therefore, all concerned students are advised to regularly visit the website for the latest updates and information.
